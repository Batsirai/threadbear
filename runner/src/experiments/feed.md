## What it does

A Feed button in the activity bar (and Go > Show Feed) shows your Inbox in the main panel as one roomy card per thread, one after another, instead of a row each in the sidebar: those that need you first, then the running ones, then the rest, each newest first. Hibernating and archived threads aren't in it, and the sidebar stays as it is. A card starts with what you asked, your first message to the thread (for a thread you didn't start, who started it: another thread, a schedule or a tool, then its title). Under that, "Where it's at" is the latest turn's headline and when it was last updated, with its questions drawn as the thread's own buttons and rows, Recommended included, to answer right there: the button you press spins and the others wait until the thread has the answer. A thread that's working says what it's doing with a spinner instead. The footer's fold ("Claude · 9 messages") opens the thread's recent messages inside the card, each cut to three lines with See full and Open thread. Done archives the thread and shrinks its card to one line with Undo until you leave the Feed; Later is the usual hibernate menu. A card that needs you, or whose messages you opened, has a box to reply to the thread from there. When new cards come in while you're scrolled down, a "↑ 3 new" pill at the top takes you back up.

A box at the top, "Start something new…", starts a thread; its card comes in at the top. With its For everyone switch on (the marks of the labs signed in on this Mac), the question goes to Claude, Codex, Gemini and Grok at once: Claude's thread leads, each other lab answers in a thread of its own (on its default model at medium effort, or the model your Code review lists name for it), and every answer is passed to the lead, whose reply sums up where they stand. Its card says "for everyone" at the top; "Where it's at" is the lead's summing up and its asks, with who's still thinking, who's waiting for you in their own thread, and any lab left out and why (not signed in on this Mac, no login with room, or its thread didn't start). The fold ("Claude, Codex, Gemini and Grok · 3 replies") opens every lab's replies in time order, each with its mark, See full and Open thread to its own thread, and your replies between. The reply box's chip sends to @everyone or, clicked on, to one lab. The members' threads aren't cards of their own, and put themselves away once they've answered. On a card you started, Ask the others puts your first question to the other labs and makes it such a card. Done puts its members away too. Desktop (GPUI) app only, for threads on this Mac's runner.

## Lives in

The page in `desktop/gpui/src/inbox_feed.rs` (cards, fold, reply box, Done and Undo, the new pill, its dump and script command `feed`), opened as `SidebarMode::Feed` in `shell.rs` (Go > Show Feed, the main panel, back and forward) with its button in `rails.rs`; its order, card states and words in `desktop/core/src/feed.rs`. The asks reuse the Decisions card (`DecisionCard::for_feed` in `project_decisions.rs`) and `AskHost::ask_sending` and `sending_button` in `composer_card.rs`. The card's top line is the summary's `request` (docs/protocol.md, "What Mike first asked"), set in `runner/src/runner.ts` (`requestLine`, `backfillRequest`) and carried to other Macs on `threadIndex` rows from backend API 96. A `[Picture N]` in it, and in Mike's messages in the fold, is drawn as his bubble draws it, a chip among the words and the picture as a tile that opens the viewer: the request's pictures are read from the thread's first events (`feed_read_request`, `request_images`), and the chips and tiles are the bubble's own (`chip` and `lay_out` in `chips.rs`, `picture_grid` in `rows/images.rs`, another Mac's through `remote_pictures.rs`). The Feed draws the picture viewer over itself (`render_feed`), so the side pane leaves it then (`highlights.rs`). For everyone: `threads.create`'s `everyone` and `threads.askOthers` (docs/protocol/threads.md, "For everyone") in `runner/src/runner/everyone.ts` (members, the answers to the lead, the labs left out), the `tellLead` call in `completeTurn` (`runner.ts`), `everyoneAnswers` on queued messages and `everyone` on their `user.message`, the summary's `everyone` and `everyoneOf`, `reviewPicker` exported from `lab-review.ts`, the `everyoneOf` line in `quiet-inbox.ts`, `everyone` refused from a thread's key in `rpc-gate.ts`, and the `threads.askOthers` row in `parity.ts`; in the app the top box, the panel card, its fold and reply chip and Ask the others in `inbox_feed.rs`, the panel rules (`panel`, `with_members`, `panel_messages`, `reply_targets`, `can_ask_others`) in `core/src/feed.rs`, and `everyone`/`everyoneOf` on the core `ThreadSummary`. The mock runner's `TB_MOCK_FEED` demo (with Grok and Gemini logins and a panel card); `features/feed.feature`. The app learns it's on from `hello`'s `experiments`.

Delete `inbox_feed.rs`, `core/src/feed.rs`, `features/feed.feature` and its steps, `SidebarMode::Feed` and its arms, `Command::ShowFeed` and its Go menu item, the rail's Feed button, the `inbox_feed` field on `Threadbear`, `leave_feed_for_thread` and its calls, the Feed line in `leave_selection`, `DecisionCard::for_feed` with `keeps_picks` and `sending_now`, and `ask_sending` with `sending_button`.
