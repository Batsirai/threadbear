## About

An agent tells you it found three hotels, opened a pull request or read
something in the docs, and then you go looking for each of them yourself. Or
it does give links, in a pile called Sources at the bottom, to home pages and
search results.

With this on, an agent links whatever you might want to look at without being
asked: every option when you're choosing between real things, every PR, issue,
doc, dashboard and file, and the page a fact came from. The link sits on the
name of the thing and goes to the page you'd use, such as the listing with your
dates. If it can't find a real link it says so.

It's a habit, not a job: nothing runs on a schedule. While it's on, every
thread's agent is told the lines below.

## Example

Without it:

*The best three are Harbour House, The Lantern and Pier 9. Harbour House is the
cheapest. Sources: booking sites, review sites.*

With it:

| Hotel | Per night | Walk to the venue |
|---|---|---|
| [Harbour House](https://example.com/harbour-house?in=2027-03-04&out=2027-03-07) | $182 | 6 min |
| [The Lantern](https://example.com/the-lantern?in=2027-03-04&out=2027-03-07) | $205 | 2 min |
| [Pier 9](https://example.com/pier-9?in=2027-03-04&out=2027-03-07) | $240 | 11 min |

I'd take The Lantern. I couldn't find a page for Pier 9's parking, so that
price is from its phone line.

## Habit

Link anything the user might want to open, without being asked. Most of all when they're choosing between real things (hotels, flights, products, services, venues): every option gets its own link, on its name, including in a comparison table. Link the page they'd actually use (the listing or booking page with their dates, the product page), not a search page or a home page, and not a pile of sources at the end. The same goes for PRs, issues, docs, dashboards, files and the page a fact came from. If you can't find a real link for something, say so rather than leaving it bare.
